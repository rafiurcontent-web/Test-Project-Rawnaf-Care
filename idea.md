Rawnaf Care – Idea Proposition

Project Name: Rawnaf Care

Problem:
In Bangladesh, expecting mothers, parents, and general health-conscious individuals struggle to find reliable health information and easy-to-use health tools in one place. Most available information is scattered and not backed by a trusted, identifiable medical professional.

Solution:
Rawnaf Care is a health-focused website centered around Dr. Nafia Islam's expertise, combining her blog content, video advice, and a set of interactive health calculator tools in a single platform. Visitors can read trustworthy health articles, watch expert video guidance, and get instant, science-based health insights — all without any signup friction.

Target Users:

Expecting mothers and new parents
Parents concerned about their child's health and growth
General health-conscious individuals

Core Features (MVP):

Admin System
Admin login/authentication
Admin dashboard to manage blog posts and videos
Blog Section
List and detail view of Dr. Nafia's health articles
Public access, no login required
Health Advice Video
YouTube video list with modal popup playback
Public access, no login required
Health Tools (calculate-only, no data persistence)
BMI Calculator (WHO + South Asian standards)
Fertile Window Calculator
Diabetes Risk Calculator (South Asian cutoffs)
Child Growth Tracker (instant calculation, no history saved)
About/Credentials Page
Dr. Nafia's professional background and qualifications
Appointment callout card with WhatsApp contact button (prefilled message via wa.me link)
Admin manually follows up to confirm appointments

User Access Model:

No signup/login required for general users — all content and tools are freely accessible to maximize reach and minimize friction
A dedicated mobile app (future phase) will introduce full user accounts for personalized features like appointment booking and order history

Tech Stack:

Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind CSS
Backend: Node.js + Express
Database: MongoDB Atlas

Out of Scope (future phases):

Smart Diet Planner
E-book Store
Rawnaf Mart (e-commerce)
Full appointment booking system (form + slot management + payment verification)
Pregnancy Tracker
Growth history tracking (with graphs)
General user signup/login

Definition of Success (MVP):
A visitor can browse blog content, watch videos, and use at least four health calculators to get instant, accurate results — with no crashes or errors — while the admin can independently manage all blog and video content through the dashboard.
